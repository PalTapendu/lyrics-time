TRANSPARENCY & LOCAL PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. 100% Local Processing (Zero Cloud Uploads)

SyncLines is built from the ground up as a fully client-side web application. When you open or drag-and-drop an audio file (such as MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, or OGG) or paste lyric text into the editor:

Your files never leave your computer. All audio decoding, spectrum analysis, waveform peak computation, and subtitle formatting (.LRC, .SRT, .VTT, .TXT) are performed strictly inside your browser via the Web Audio API and JavaScript. No audio is ever uploaded to any server.

2. Browser Storage (localStorage & File Handles)

To ensure you do not lose your work if your tab refreshes, SyncLines uses standard in-browser localStorage and the native browser File System Access API. The data stored includes:

  • synclines-editor-theme: Remembers your interface theme preference (Light or Dark).
  • timing-console-autosave: Temporarily caches your current song title, lyric rows, and timestamps.
  • timing-console-history: Saves references to your recent session files for 1-click reopening.
  • timing-console-clicks: Remembers whether rhythmic audio click feedback is toggled on or off.
  • timing-console-dock-h: Remembers your preferred height for the docked waveform player.

How to clear this data: You can purge all stored project data at any time by clicking "Clear Session" within the editor, or by clearing your browser's site data/cookies under your browser's privacy settings.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Resources

SyncLines fetches static font files and libraries from reputable public CDNs:

  • Google Fonts: Typefaces (Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono, Caveat) are loaded from Google servers to render typography.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs: Open-source helper libraries (Three.js and GSAP) are loaded from Cloudflare to drive GPU-accelerated canvas spectrum animations.

These CDNs receive standard anonymous HTTP request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) necessary to serve the static font and script assets.

4. Optional Analytics

By default, SyncLines operates with zero analytics, zero telemetry, and zero tracking cookies. If the site operator enables optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4), IP anonymization is enforced and only general aggregated usage telemetry (such as page views and browser types) is recorded. You may block analytics using any standard content blocker or browser privacy extension.
